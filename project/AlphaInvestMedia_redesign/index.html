About Alpha Invest Media
16 years of trusted construction delivery

Alpha Invest Media Kft. has 16 years of experience delivering reliable, professional construction services. As a general contractor, we cover a wide portfolio across industry and commerce, serving partners who want to grow, renew, and build with confidence.

Clients value having one trusted company manage every phase of a project. Our full-service approach means design, planning and execution stay aligned under a single team, which speeds delivery and simplifies communication.

We aim to exceed expectations for every new and existing partner, building long-term trust and strong relationships with every completed project.
